About

Behavioural science
meets real-world AI.

I am a researcher and analyst with a background in behavioural economics, cognitive psychology, and data science. For nearly three years I have worked at the Muthukrishna Lab at LSE, contributing to UNDP research on AI and human development.

My dissertation examined algorithmic aversion in financial decision-making — how overconfidence and moral judgement interact when people choose whether to delegate to a robo-advisor. I am also a published co-author in the Journal of Human Development and Capabilities.

I am a Nova Talent member, IE High Potential Award recipient, and hold two Academic Excellence Awards from LSE in Economics and Psychology.

Algorithmic Aversion in Robo-Advisory Contexts
Behavioural Economics · Financial Decision-Making
